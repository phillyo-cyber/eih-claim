Game day claim

Fill one form after the game. It writes your IHUK claim sheet and drops the match fee, mileage and any extras straight into ExpenseIn as drafts, ready for you to check and submit.

You set this up once on each device. After that it's one tap on the ExpenseIn page whenever you've a game to claim.

  • Add the button, once

    Follow the steps below for your phone or laptop. Takes a minute, and you never do it again on that device.

  • Open ExpenseIn and tap it

    Sign into ExpenseIn as normal, then tap Game day claim. Your form opens on top.

  • Fill in the game, tap Create

    Pick the rink and teams, snap any receipts. It builds every draft for you. Nothing is submitted until you say so.

Step 2 · add it to your device

  1. In Safari, open any page, then tap the share icon and choose Add Bookmark. Save it. The page itself doesn't matter - you're about to replace its address.
  2. Tap the bookmarks icon, then Edit. Tap the bookmark you just made.
  3. Rename it Game day claim. Delete the web address underneath and paste the button link from Step 1 in its place. Tap Done.
  4. To use it: open ExpenseIn and sign in, tap the bookmarks icon, and tap Game day claim. The form opens on top of the page.
Tip: after the first use, tap Share → Add to Home Screen on ExpenseIn so it opens full-screen like an app.
  1. In Chrome, open any page and tap the ⋮ menu, then the star to bookmark it.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Bookmarks, find the new one and tap Edit (pencil icon).
  3. Rename it Game day claim, clear the URL field and paste the button link from Step 1. Save.
  4. To use it: open ExpenseIn and sign in, then tap the address bar, type Game day claim, and tap the bookmark that appears in the suggestions.
On Android you must run it from the address bar suggestion, not the bookmarks list - Chrome blocks these links when opened from the list.
  1. Make sure the bookmarks bar is showing: Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+B toggles it.
  2. Right-click the bookmarks bar and choose Add page…
  3. Name it Game day claim, and in the URL field paste the button link from Step 1. Save.
  4. To use it: open ExpenseIn and sign in, then click Game day claim on the bookmarks bar. The form opens on top of the page.
Same button works in Edge and Brave. On a laptop you can also drag the receipt files in instead of taking a photo.

What officials should know

Your login stays with ExpenseIn. The form runs inside your own signed-in ExpenseIn session on your device. It never asks for your password and nothing is sent to another site.
Nothing is submitted for you. It only creates drafts. You review each one in ExpenseIn and submit when you're ready - once a week, as EIH asks.
Your details are remembered on your own device only, so next time you just enter the game.
